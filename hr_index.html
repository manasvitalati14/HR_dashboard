Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard project was developed to analyze workforce performance, employee attrition, salary trends and departmental insights. The project demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow beginning with data storage in MySQL, exploratory data analysis using Python, interactive visualization in Power BI and documentation using HTML and CSS.

The dashboard enables management to monitor workforce statistics, employee performance, salary distribution, overtime trends, training participation and employee retention, helping support data-driven HR decision making.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze workforce demographics and employee distribution.
  • Measure employee attrition across departments.
  • Evaluate salary trends by department and job role.
  • Monitor employee performance and promotion history.
  • Identify overtime and work-life balance patterns.
  • Create interactive dashboards for HR decision making.
  • Provide business recommendations based on HR metrics.

Business Insights

  • The organization maintains a balanced workforce across multiple business departments.
  • Employee attrition remains comparatively low, indicating good workforce stability.
  • Departments with experienced employees generally show higher average salaries.
  • Employees with higher performance scores are more likely to receive promotions.
  • Training programs contribute positively to employee performance and productivity.
  • Most employees maintain a satisfactory work-life balance.
  • Overtime is concentrated in selected departments and should be monitored regularly.
  • Average employee experience indicates a healthy mix of junior and experienced professionals.

Recommendations

  1. Continue employee engagement activities to maintain low attrition.
  2. Provide additional technical and soft-skill training for employees with lower performance scores.
  3. Review department-wise salary structures annually to ensure market competitiveness.
  4. Monitor overtime and workload distribution to improve employee well-being.
  5. Increase internal promotion opportunities to improve employee motivation.
  6. Use dashboard insights for workforce planning and hiring decisions.

The HR Analytics Dashboard successfully demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow beginning with data storage in MySQL, analytical querying using SQL, data exploration with Python, dashboard development using Power BI and final documentation using HTML and CSS.

These insights help HR professionals make informed decisions regarding employee retention, resource planning and organizational performance.
